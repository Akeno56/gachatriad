# Published cards — approved v2

`data/cards/catalog.json` contains **814** approved cards. IDs include universe prefixes and preserve alternate forms. Forty-one unscored characters remain artwork-only. Release hashes, exclusions, approvals and Aha's exact A/8/A/2 override are recorded in `data/cards/release.json`. See [v0.12 source assumptions and pool membership](RELEASE_V12.md).

The new 413 cards are Epic Seven (362) and Star Savior (51); the other addition is Aha. Universe I contains Genshin, Star Rail, Zenless and Wuthering Waves. Universe II contains Epic Seven, Star Savior, NTE and Endfield. Importing metadata does not silently publish cards: proposals and promotion remain separate commands. The override file preserves Aha when proposals regenerate.

Fresh profiles receive three exclusive Starter packs. The v0.12 maintenance reset grants the same three packs to retained accounts. Each pack has five cards, one 3★+ guarantee, no 5★ and 1% Holo. Paid-pack odds and prices are in [the economy guide](BOOSTER_PACKS.md).

Holo variants add exactly one point to an eligible side below A. Acquisition rolls the side once; stable variant IDs preserve it through inventory, decks, receipts and transfers. They remain the same base character for deck uniqueness. The album previews one default Holo orientation for unowned characters and displays exact owned orientations. Holo cannot be purchased directly.

The local assets include 855 portraits, 845 icons and 444 listings across eight universes. Source URLs, hashes and provenance stay in import metadata; cards have no per-character source links. Prydwen, HideoutGacha, game owners and music contributors are credited on `/credits`. No AI character/background illustrations are included.
