# Implemented game rules

## v0.4 additions

- **Elemental:** choose one to five distinct marked cells per match, each assigned one of Fire, Water, Ice, Lightning, Wind, Earth, Holy or Poison. This count distribution is our implementation choice; it is not a claim about FFVIII's exact RNG probabilities. A matching card gets +1 on every side; mismatched and unaffiliated cards get −1. Neutral cells do nothing. Values can effectively reach 0 or 11, while the printed card remains unchanged. The modifier stays with the card/cell after ownership changes. Same/Plus use printed ranks; standard comparisons and Combo chains use the adjusted ranks. Cells are generated by the server once, persisted and shared by both players. See the user's [FFVIII guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2935597806).
- **Custom Combo:** an explicit checkbox, usable with Same or Plus. Trade One requires Closed visibility and all three of Same, Plus and Combo. Adding Random or Elemental keeps Trade One available; removing a prerequisite clears/disables it. Server validation rejects incompatible stake requests. Old incompatible waiting tables are closed; historical matches retain their snapshots.
- **Onboarding:** players with zero completed games must finish the short tutorial or acknowledge the rulebook before starting/joining a duel. The acknowledgement persists across reload and guest registration. Existing players with completed games are not forced through it. Existing active matches remain resumable.
- **Cosmetics:** standard/Holo definitions share stats. Deck uniqueness is checked by base character identity. Five-star framing and Holo animation never affect capture rules, element bonuses or rarity restrictions.

## v0.3 modes and ownership

Unranked now defaults to **Three Open**: the server randomly reveals three cards in each hand, with the other two hidden until played or the game finishes. Reveal identities stay fixed as cards leave the hand. All Open and Three Open cannot combine. Hidden cards are redacted from authenticated API responses, including their IDs and values; the historical local-only presentation described below is superseded.

Ranked uses closed hands, **Same, Plus, Combo and Trade One**. Its winner selects exactly one card from the loser's original five-card deck, including the unplayed card. Draws transfer nothing; forfeits retain the stakes. The choice expires after two minutes, followed by server selection. Copies are reserved and settlement is transactional and idempotent. No card is transferred merely because it was captured on the board.

Custom lobbies share one public, filterable browser displaying 1/2 waiting or 2/2 playing tables and all selected rules. Random selects a legal hand from each player's available collection. Draft and Roulette use separate queues. Roulette selects compatible visibility/Same/Plus/Combo/Random rules at match start, with no trade. Draft automatically opens three temporary five-card boosters per player, then allows five legal cards from the fifteen offers; it uses Three Open, Same, Plus, Combo, no trade and a separate rating. See ARENA.md for the current Draft design.

Pending Trade One settlement blocks both players from starting another match until the winner chooses a card or the two-minute automatic claim resolves. The winner is prompted to return to selection when navigating away. Completed multiplayer results support a rematch with the same opponent/rules; humans must both agree, while idle bots accept automatically. A lost last copy must still be replaced in the active deck before starting a constructed rematch.

All owned decks contain five different enabled cards: at most one 5★ and one 4★, or two 4★ without a 5★. Online turns allow 60 seconds. Missing a turn plays automatically; three missed turns forfeit. Practice retains no human turn deadline. Timers and matches persist across reload/restart. Labeled local bots use these same rules.

## Foundation and resolution policy

Primary reference: [FFXIV official Triple Triad guide](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/playguide/contentsguide/goldsaucer/tripletriad/), consulted 7 November 2026. The nine-card board, tenth-card scoring, Same/Plus friendly-neighbor participation, and Combo behavior follow this reference. The explicit simultaneous resolution policy below makes ambiguous intersections reproducible.

## Standard

Two players each bring five distinct card definitions. A uniformly chosen first player starts. Each turn places exactly one hand card into an empty cell on a 3×3 board. Values are ordered **top, right, bottom, left**, range 1–10, and display 10 as **A**. Compare only orthogonally adjacent touching sides. A strictly greater attacking value captures an opposing card. Equal values do not capture normally. Ownership changes; identity and directional values do not. Cards never rotate.

After nine placements, count board ownership plus each player's remaining hand. Total score stays 10. More than 5 wins; 5–5 draws. No turn timers or surrender rewards exist in local practice.

## Special rules

- **All Open:** show both hands. Three Open reveals three selected cards in each starting hand; closed hands hide them all. Opponent identities and stats are redacted by the server until revealed or played.
- **Same:** at least two occupied adjacent cards have equal touching values. Friendly neighbors count toward the trigger; flip only matching enemies. A friendly card that was already yours does not initiate a chain.
- **Plus:** group occupied adjacent cards by the sum of the two touching values. Every group of at least two qualifies. Friendly neighbors count toward the trigger; only enemies flip. Multiple sum groups can trigger simultaneously.
- **Combo:** newly captured Same/Plus cards attack adjacent enemies using the ordinary strictly-greater rule. New Combo captures repeat this process. Ordinary captures and already-friendly cards do not initiate the chain. Combo requires Same or Plus.

## Resolution order

1. Validate status, turn, cell, occupancy, and card possession.
2. Place the chosen card. Take a pre-capture snapshot of adjacent values.
3. Evaluate Same and Plus independently against that same snapshot. Union their targets. If one card qualifies for both, label its event `same`; it is captured once.
4. Flip special enemy targets and enqueue only those new captures.
5. Resolve ordinary captures from the placed card. These do not enter the combo queue.
6. If enabled, process the queue breadth-first. For each queued card check top, right, bottom, then left. Enqueue new Combo captures. A flipped card cannot be captured again in this move.
7. Remove the hand card, append move and capture events, change turn, then detect board completion.

Special calculations use face values. Future modifiers must be added at a documented effective-value boundary with explicit tests. No special rule is inferred from a character's element or artwork.

## Presets

| Preset       | Rules                            |
| ------------ | -------------------------------- |
| Classic      | Standard captures + All Open     |
| Standard     | Standard captures + closed hands |
| Same Clash   | All Open + Same + Combo          |
| Plus Battle  | All Open + Plus + Combo          |
| Combo Master | All Open + Same + Plus + Combo   |

FFXIV treats Combo as a consequence of Same/Plus. These presets do too; the engine exposes a separate switch for targeted testing and future configuration.

Same Wall, Elemental, Reverse, Fallen Ace, Order, Chaos, Sudden Death, Ascension, Descension and Swap are not implemented. Sudden Death will require a documented rematch cap.

## Reproducible Combo lesson

The title menu's **Combo lesson** replays March 7th to C1, Chixia to B3, Yangyang to B1, Endministrator to A1, Amber to A3 and Anby to B2 with the published catalog values. Before the solution, A1, B1, B2 and B3 belong to the opponent.

Place Billy at A2. His left 5 + Endministrator's right 3 = 8, and bottom 4 + Anby's top 4 = 8. **Plus** captures both. Anby's right 4 beats Chixia's left 2 at B3 (**Combo**); Endministrator's bottom 9 beats Yangyang's top 5 at B1 (**Combo**). Exactly four cards flip, two through Combo.

This is verified in both engine and browser tests. Combo existed in the engine; the new presentation makes its trigger and sequence visible through delayed flips, reason badges, a callout, and sound.
